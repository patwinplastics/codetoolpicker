What happened, in order
  • July 14, 2025: Cognition, the company behind the Devin coding agent, signed a definitive agreement to acquire Windsurf, the agentic IDE. Official posts: Cognition's announcement and Windsurf's "The Next Chapter". TechCrunch reported the same day.
  • June 2, 2026: Cognition renamed Windsurf to Devin Desktop and made the Agent Command Center the default view. Same IDE, same plans and pricing, delivered as an over-the-air update. Source: Windsurf is now Devin Desktop.
  • Nov 12, 2026: Cognition updated the Teams plan: 100-member cap (down from 200), SSO and cost controls, Devin Desktop on every seat, 2 concurrent Free-mode SWE-2 cloud sessions per member, a standard BAA, and a claim that free SWE-2 in Desktop and CLI is now a permanent part of Teams, Pro and Max. Source: Devin's new Teams plan.
  • Nov 13, 2026: Cognition launched Devin Apps: private web apps that Devin builds and serves from its cloud machine at a stable, sign-in-required URL. Apps are never public. Billing is based on how long the machine is awake. Docs say the feature is rolling out gradually.
  • Nov 18, 2026: Teams became a flat $80 a month for the whole team, paid in as shared on-demand credits, for up to 100 members. The $40 per full seat fee is gone from the pricing page and the billing docs (re-checked Nov 26, 2026).
  • As of Nov 15, 2026: windsurf.com still redirects to devin.ai/desktop. There is one self-serve price list at devin.ai/pricing. We re-checked the Nov 12 Teams facts and the SWE-2 docs conflict (see what we could not pin down).

What five people actually pay on Teams

Since Nov 18, 2026, the pricing page lists Teams at a flat $80 a month, and the self-serve billing docs say every Teams subscription is $80 a month, charged as prepaid on-demand credits shared by the whole team. There is no per-seat fee and no per-member allowance: all usage draws from that pool, and admins can top it up or set auto-reload.

Before Nov 18, Teams was $40 per full seat with an $80 monthly minimum, so five full seats cost $200. Now the plan price is $80 for any team size up to 100 members, and what a busy team really pays depends on how many extra credits it buys. Free-mode SWE-2 cloud sessions (2 at a time per member) don't use credits. Teams accounts already over 100 members keep their current members but cannot add new ones until they are under 100.

What changed for teams on Nov 12, 2026

Cognition's Teams plan post, re-checked against the pricing page and billing docs on Nov 15, 2026:

  • Devin Desktop for every teammate. You no longer buy a separate Desktop seat. Every member gets Desktop, CLI and cloud sessions. (Since Nov 18 the whole plan is a flat $80 a month in shared credits, with no per-seat fee.)
  • SSO and cost controls. SAML or OIDC (Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, or another compliant provider). Once SSO is on, you can turn off email login. Admins can set up to 3 usage tiers per member. On Teams, SSO signs in existing members only, so invite people before they use SSO. SCIM provisioning is Enterprise-only.
  • Free-mode cloud sessions. Each Teams member can run up to 2 concurrent Devin cloud sessions in Free mode, powered by SWE-2, at no cost. To start a third, wait for one to finish or pick another mode.
  • Free SWE-2 on Desktop and CLI for Pro, Teams and Max. The Nov 12 blog calls this a permanent part of those plans, and the pricing page lists "Free use of SWE-2" under Pro with no end date. The models docs still give a Nov 30, 2026 cutoff. See the conflict.
  • A standard BAA for Teams customers, requested from Settings, without an enterprise contract.
  • 100-member cap, down from 200. Accounts already over 100 keep their current members but cannot add new ones until they are under 100.
  • Quota seats are retiring in 30 days from the Nov 12 post, so around Dec 12, 2026. The Nov 26 billing docs no longer mention full, flex or quota seats at all: Teams is one flat plan with shared credits.

Devin Apps, launched Nov 13, 2026

The day after the Teams update, Cognition launched Devin Apps. A Devin App is a private web app that Devin builds and keeps serving from its own cloud machine. You describe the tool, Devin puts it online at a stable URL, and teammates open it after signing in to Devin. Apps are never public. Typical uses are internal tools, dashboards, and a beta or staging copy of your product.

This sits on the Cloud side of the choice, not in the local IDE. If you mainly want Tab, inline edits and a desktop editor, Apps do not change that. If you want Devin to stand up a private tool your team can keep opening from a link, that is a reason to be on a plan that includes Devin Cloud, and to look for Apps in the sidebar.

The machine sleeps when nobody is using the App (the docs say 5 minutes after the last request) and wakes on the next visit; the first load after a sleep can take a few seconds. Billing is based on how long the machine is awake, so an unused App costs nothing while it sleeps. Building and editing an App is billed as a normal Devin session. An App runs on one machine, so Cognition says it is a fit for internal tools and dashboards, not for high-traffic production services.

Cognition has not published a per-hour rate, or a list of which plans include Apps. The docs say Apps are rolling out gradually, so you may not see them in the sidebar yet. Re-checked Nov 15, 2026.

Who should pick which
  • You searched "Devin vs Windsurf" as if they compete. They don't, not anymore. Start with Devin Desktop if you want an AI IDE. Add or use Devin Cloud when you want long-running tasks on a cloud machine. Pro at $20 includes both.
  • You already use Windsurf. You already have Devin Desktop. Cognition says the update keeps your plan, pricing, extensions, settings and in-progress work. You do not need a new subscription just because of the rename.
  • You want Tab, inline edits and a familiar VS Code-style IDE. Download Devin Desktop. The Free plan is enough to try Tab and light agent use.
  • You want an agent that keeps working after you close the laptop. That is Devin Cloud. On Pro, Max or Teams it is part of the same plan, and Desktop can hand a session to the cloud with the same context.
  • You want Devin to host a private internal tool or staging preview. That is Devin Apps, launched Nov 13. It runs on Devin's cloud machine, not in the local IDE, and everyone who opens it has to sign in to Devin.
  • You need centralized admin, SSO and spend caps. Teams, not a pile of individual Pro plans (Pro and Max cannot be shared). See the Nov 12 admin features.
  • You are choosing an AI IDE against Cursor. That is a real comparison: Cursor Plus is $8 but Grok-only; Devin Pro is $20 with frontier models in the quota. Full breakdown: Cursor vs Windsurf (Devin Desktop).
  • You mainly want Cognition's own coding models. SWE-2 and SWE-2 Flash run in Desktop and CLI (Flash is not in Devin Cloud). See SWE-2 Flash vs SWE-2, and the caveat below about the free-until date.

What we could not pin down

The Nov 12 Teams post says Cognition is "making free SWE-2 in Devin Desktop and CLI a permanent part of our Teams, Pro, and Max plans." The pricing page now lists "Free use of SWE-2" under Pro with no end date, which matches the blog more than the docs. The models docs still say SWE-2 is free on self-serve Desktop and CLI through Nov 30, 2026, then $0.50 per million input tokens, $2.50 output and $0.05 cache reads from Dec 1 to Dec 31, 2026, then $3, $15 and $0.30, and that this does not apply to Devin cloud sessions. We are not treating the Nov 30 cutoff as cancelled until the docs catch up.
  • SWE-2 Flash is still documented as free until Dec 4, 2026 at 18:00 UTC, then $0.10 / $0.01 / $0.125 / $0.50 per million input, cache read, cache write and output. Medium is on Free; High and Max need a paid plan. Desktop and CLI only, not Cloud.
  • Quota sizes for Pro and Max are not published as numbers, only as "increased" or "significantly higher," plus daily and weekly refresh rules.
  • How far $80 of Teams credits goes. Cognition doesn't publish what a typical session costs in credits, so we can't say how many active members $80 covers before you need to top up.
  • Devin Apps pricing and plan availability are unpublished. The blog and docs say billing is based on machine uptime, and that building or editing an App is normal session work, but there is no per-hour rate and no list of which plans include Apps.

FAQ

9 questions

Are Devin and Windsurf the same thing?

They are now the same product family, not competing tools. Cognition acquired Windsurf on July 14, 2025, and renamed the Windsurf IDE to Devin Desktop on June 2, 2026. windsurf.com now redirects to devin.ai/desktop. "Devin" also still refers to the cloud software-engineering agent (Devin Cloud), which is a different surface from the IDE.

Is Windsurf still available?

Yes, under a new name. Cognition says Devin Desktop is the same IDE, editor and features, delivered as a standard over-the-air update, with your plan, pricing, extensions and settings carried over. The Windsurf plugin for JetBrains IDEs is still offered under the Windsurf name, per the Desktop page FAQ.

What is Devin Desktop?

Cognition's downloadable AI IDE, formerly Windsurf. It is a full IDE (editor, extensions, keybindings, LSPs, workflows) with an Agent Command Center for local and cloud agents. You can use it with local-only agents; you do not have to use Devin Cloud.

What is Devin Cloud?

Cognition's autonomous, long-running software-engineering agent that runs on its own machine in the cloud. On self-serve Pro, Max and Teams it is included and shares the same usage quota as Devin Desktop and Devin CLI. The Desktop FAQ still describes Devin Cloud as a separate SKU if you are on a legacy Enterprise or Cognition Platform plan.

How much do they cost?

One self-serve list, re-checked Nov 26, 2026: Free $0, Pro $20 a month, Max $200 a month, Teams a flat $80 a month for up to 100 members, paid in as shared on-demand credits (no per-seat fee since Nov 18). No annual price is listed. Enterprise is sales-led. The pricing page lists free use of SWE-2 on Pro; the models docs still give a Nov 30, 2026 cutoff (see the conflict).

I already pay for Windsurf. Do I need a new plan?

No. Cognition says existing Windsurf plans, including legacy Enterprise, keep the same pricing, and the Devin Desktop update is a rename plus a new default Agent Command Center view. Settings are ported automatically.

Which is better for teams?

Use Teams if more than one person needs a shared bill. Pro and Max are single-user plans and cannot be shared. As of Nov 12, 2026, Teams is capped at 100 members, every member gets Desktop, the plan is a flat $80 a month in shared credits with no per-seat fee (since Nov 18), each member gets up to 2 concurrent Free-mode SWE-2 cloud sessions, and admins get SAML or OIDC SSO, 3-tier cost controls, centralized billing, and a standard BAA on request.

Is SWE-2 permanently free on Desktop and CLI?

Unconfirmed. The Nov 12 blog and the current pricing page present free SWE-2 on Pro, Max and Teams with no end date. The models docs, whose tables are still dated Nov 9, still say the self-serve Desktop and CLI window ends Nov 30, 2026, and that it does not apply to Devin cloud sessions. We are not treating the cutoff as cancelled until the docs catch up.

What are Devin Apps?

Private web apps, launched Nov 13, 2026, that Devin builds and serves from its cloud machine at a stable URL. Everyone who opens an App has to sign in to Devin, and Apps are never public. Billing is based on how long the machine is awake. Cognition has not published a per-hour rate or which plans include Apps, and the docs say the feature is rolling out gradually. More detail.
