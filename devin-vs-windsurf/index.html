Devin vs Windsurf, Nov 2026: they're not rivals anymore

Short answer: Cognition acquired Windsurf on July 14, 2025 and renamed the Windsurf IDE to Devin Desktop on June 2, 2026. windsurf.com now redirects to devin.ai/desktop. The useful choice is not Devin versus Windsurf. It is whether you want the local IDE (Devin Desktop), the autonomous cloud agent (Devin Cloud), or both. On self-serve Pro ($20), Max ($200) and Teams, you get both under one quota.

Checked Nov 13, 2026 on Cognition's official pricing page, docs and blogs. Prices change often; confirm with the linked sources.

What happened, in order

  • July 14, 2025: Cognition, the company behind the Devin coding agent, signed a definitive agreement to acquire Windsurf, the agentic IDE. Official posts: Cognition's announcement and Windsurf's "The Next Chapter". TechCrunch reported the same day.
  • June 2, 2026: Cognition renamed Windsurf to Devin Desktop and made the Agent Command Center the default view. Same IDE, same plans and pricing, delivered as an over-the-air update. Source: Windsurf is now Devin Desktop.
  • Today: windsurf.com redirects to devin.ai/desktop (we checked the redirect on Nov 13, 2026). There is one self-serve price list at devin.ai/pricing.
  • Nov 12, 2026: Cognition updated the Teams plan: 100-member cap (down from 200), SSO and cost controls, Devin Desktop on every seat, Free-mode SWE-2 cloud sessions, and a standard BAA. Source: Devin's new Teams plan.

What changed for teams on Nov 12, 2026

Cognition's Teams plan post, confirmed against the pricing page and billing docs on Nov 13:

  • Devin Desktop for every teammate. You no longer buy a separate Desktop seat. Full and flex seats both include Desktop, CLI and cloud sessions.
  • SSO and cost controls. SAML or OIDC (Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, or another compliant provider). Once SSO is on, you can turn off email login. Admins can set up to 3 usage tiers per member. On Teams, SSO signs in existing members only, so invite people before they use SSO. SCIM provisioning is Enterprise-only.
  • Free-mode cloud sessions. Each Teams member can run up to 2 concurrent Devin cloud sessions in Free mode, powered by SWE-2, at no cost.
  • A standard BAA for Teams customers, requested from Settings, without an enterprise contract.
  • 100-member cap, down from 200. Cognition also said it is retiring "quota seats" on Teams in 30 days (from the Nov 12 post, so around mid-December 2026). It did not spell out, in that post, how quota seats map to today's full and flex seats.

Who should pick which

  • You searched "Devin vs Windsurf" as if they compete. They don't, not anymore. Start with Devin Desktop if you want an AI IDE. Add or use Devin Cloud when you want long-running tasks on a cloud machine. Pro at $20 includes both.
  • You already use Windsurf. You already have Devin Desktop. Cognition says the update keeps your plan, pricing, extensions, settings and in-progress work. You do not need a new subscription just because of the rename.
  • You want Tab, inline edits and a familiar VS Code-style IDE. Download Devin Desktop. The Free plan is enough to try Tab and light agent use.
  • You want an agent that keeps working after you close the laptop. That is Devin Cloud. On Pro, Max or Teams it is part of the same plan, and Desktop can hand a session to the cloud with the same context.
  • You need centralized admin, SSO and spend caps. Teams, not a pile of individual Pro plans (Pro and Max cannot be shared). See the Nov 12 admin features.
  • You are choosing an AI IDE against Cursor. That is a real comparison: Cursor Plus is $8 but Grok-only; Devin Pro is $20 with frontier models in the quota. Full breakdown: Cursor vs Windsurf (Devin Desktop).
  • You mainly want Cognition's own coding models. SWE-2 and SWE-2 Flash run in Desktop and CLI (Flash is not in Devin Cloud). See SWE-2 Flash vs SWE-2, and the caveat below about the free-until date.

What we could not pin down

  • Is SWE-2 now permanently free on paid Desktop and CLI? The Nov 12 Teams post says Cognition is "making free SWE-2 in Devin Desktop and CLI a permanent part of our Teams, Pro, and Max plans." The models docs still say SWE-2 is free on self-serve Desktop and CLI through Nov 30, 2026, then $3 per million input tokens, $15 per million output and $0.30 per million cache input, and that this does not apply to Devin cloud sessions. The docs' price tables are dated Nov 9, 2026, three days before the blog post. We are not treating the Nov 30 cutoff as cancelled until the docs catch up.
  • SWE-2 Flash is still documented as free until Dec 4, 2026 at 18:00 UTC, then $0.10 / $0.01 / $0.125 / $0.50 per million input, cache read, cache write and output. Medium is on Free; High and Max need a paid plan. Desktop and CLI only, not Cloud.
  • Quota sizes for Pro, Max and Teams full seats are not published as numbers, only as "increased" or "significantly higher," plus daily and weekly refresh rules.
  • "Quota seats" being retired in 30 days is announced, but Cognition has not published (in that post or the billing docs we read) a precise definition versus full and flex seats.

FAQ

Are Devin and Windsurf the same thing?

They are now the same product family, not competing tools. Cognition acquired Windsurf on July 14, 2025, and renamed the Windsurf IDE to Devin Desktop on June 2, 2026. windsurf.com now redirects to devin.ai/desktop. "Devin" also still refers to the cloud software-engineering agent (Devin Cloud), which is a different surface from the IDE.

Is Windsurf still available?

Yes, under a new name. Cognition says Devin Desktop is the same IDE, editor and features, delivered as a standard over-the-air update, with your plan, pricing, extensions and settings carried over. The Windsurf plugin for JetBrains IDEs is still offered under the Windsurf name, per the Desktop page FAQ.

What is Devin Desktop?

Cognition's downloadable AI IDE, formerly Windsurf. It is a full IDE (editor, extensions, keybindings, LSPs, workflows) with an Agent Command Center for local and cloud agents. You can use it with local-only agents; you do not have to use Devin Cloud.

What is Devin Cloud?

Cognition's autonomous, long-running software-engineering agent that runs on its own machine in the cloud. On self-serve Pro, Max and Teams it is included and shares the same usage quota as Devin Desktop and Devin CLI. The Desktop FAQ still describes Devin Cloud as a separate SKU if you are on a legacy Enterprise or Cognition Platform plan.

How much do they cost?

One self-serve list, checked Nov 13, 2026: Free $0, Pro $20 a month, Max $200 a month, Teams with an $80 a month minimum plus $40 a month per full seat, up to 100 members. Flex seats are free. No annual price is listed. Enterprise is sales-led.

I already pay for Windsurf. Do I need a new plan?

No. Cognition says existing Windsurf plans, including legacy Enterprise, keep the same pricing, and the Devin Desktop update is a rename plus a new default Agent Command Center view. Settings are ported automatically.

Which is better for teams?

Use Teams if more than one person needs a shared bill. Pro and Max are single-user plans and cannot be shared. As of Nov 12, 2026, Teams is capped at 100 members, every seat includes Desktop, full seats are $40, flex seats are free, and admins get SAML or OIDC SSO, 3-tier cost controls, centralized billing, and a standard BAA on request. Five full seats cost $200 a month.
