ChatGPT Business with 1 seat: is it worth it for Codex?

Short answer: OpenAI's Business help pages now say a new workspace can start with 1 paid seat. That makes Business a real option for a solo dev. But for Codex, a Standard seat gets the same usage as Plus. You pay $25 a month ($20 billed yearly) instead of $20, and the extra buys a business workspace and $20 a month in API credits, not more coding time.

If you want more Codex, the Business seat that adds usage is Premium at $125 a month ($100 billed yearly). For one person, ChatGPT Pro 100 at $100 a month gives about the same or more, for less on monthly billing. So Business only makes sense alone if you actually need what it adds: SSO, admin controls, data not used for training by default, or a workspace you plan to grow.

One catch on availability: OpenAI's own pages disagree. Three help articles say 1 seat; a separate single-seat article says it's limited to some new workspaces, and the pricing pages still say 2+ users. You find out at checkout.

Checked Nov 23, 2026 on OpenAI's Business overview, sign-up guide and billing and seats guide (all updated Nov 22 or 23), the single-seat configuration article, Business models and limits, the Business release notes, Business pricing, ChatGPT pricing, Codex pricing, API credits for ChatGPT subscribers, OpenAI's Premium seats post, Tibo Sottiaux's Nov 8 post on Pro multiples, and BleepingComputer's Oct 29 report on the Pro and Premium doubling.

The honest math

  • Standard vs Plus: same Codex, $5 more a month on monthly billing. On yearly billing it's the same $20 a month, but you pay $240 up front and can't drop it mid-term.
  • The $20 API credit is real money only if you'd spend it anyway. It works for your own apps on the OpenAI API. OpenAI says it does not cover Codex used through an API key and does not raise Codex limits.
  • Premium vs Pro 100: Premium monthly is $125 for 5x Standard (maybe about 10x after the Oct 29 doubling, unconfirmed). Pro 100 is $100 for 10x Plus. Yearly Premium matches Pro 100's $100, but with a 12-month lock-in. Unless you need the workspace features, Pro 100 is the better Codex buy.

The catches before you switch

  • Yearly means a year. OpenAI's single-seat article says a seat reduction takes effect at the start of your next billing cycle, which on an annual plan is the next annual term, and scheduling it "does not reverse current-period charges or automatically issue a refund."
  • Workspace merges are one-way. You can keep your personal ChatGPT workspace separate or merge it into Business. OpenAI says a merge "has no undo option" that moves Business content back.
  • API credits need setup. You must own both the Business workspace and the API organization, use the same email, and wait until the subscription is at least 7 days old. Credits expire at the end of each billing cycle. Yearly Business gets the full year's grant at once.
  • December changes everyone's limits. OpenAI says plan usage will have a shared limit across ChatGPT and Codex starting in December. It applies to Business as well as Plus and Pro, so moving to Business won't dodge it. No exact date is published.
  • Old Codex seats are gone. The Codex-only seat type is a legacy option for workspaces that added it before June 24, 2026. A new 1-seat workspace gets Standard or Premium only.
  • Self-serve pays by card. OpenAI says invoices, bank transfer, purchase orders and net terms aren't available on self-serve Business.

What we couldn't confirm

  • Who gets the 1-seat option. OpenAI's help pages conflict, and there's no announcement. We didn't run a checkout.
  • Premium's real multiple today. OpenAI published 5x Standard in August. Tibo's Oct 29 post said Business Premium limits were doubled, and an OpenAI release note title from that day mentions Premium, but no page gives a new number.
  • Pro multiples on an OpenAI page. 10x, 20x and 50x Plus come from Tibo's X post. OpenAI's help pages only say each Pro tier gets more than the one below.
  • The December date for the shared ChatGPT and Codex limit, and how much each plan gets under it.

FAQ

Can I buy ChatGPT Business for just one person?

Maybe. As of Nov 23, 2026, OpenAI's Business overview, sign-up and billing help pages say you can start a new ChatGPT Business workspace with 1 paid Standard or Premium seat. But OpenAI's single-seat help article says availability is limited and not every new workspace gets it, and the Business and Codex pricing pages still say 2+ users. Lower the seat count at checkout to see if 1 is allowed for you.

Does a ChatGPT Business Standard seat get more Codex usage than Plus?

No. OpenAI's Codex pricing page lists the same 5-hour estimates for Plus and Standard Business, for example 6 to 50 local messages on GPT-6.2 Astra and 17 to 180 on GPT-6.2 Sol. A Standard seat costs $25 a month, or $20 a month billed yearly, against $20 for Plus. The extra money buys business features and $20 a month in API credits, not more Codex.

Is Business Premium or ChatGPT Pro 100 better for heavy Codex use?

For one person who just wants more Codex, Pro 100 usually wins. It is $100 a month with no 5-hour limit, and OpenAI's Codex lead posted on Nov 8, 2026 that it is 10x Plus. Premium is $125 a month ($100 billed yearly) with no 5-hour limit and 5x Standard per OpenAI's August launch post; OpenAI's Codex lead said on Oct 29 that Business Premium limits were doubled, but OpenAI has not published a new multiple. Pick Premium only if you need the Business workspace features.

Do the Business API credits pay for Codex?

No. OpenAI gives $20 a month per paid Standard seat and $100 per paid Premium seat in API credits, pooled and capped at $500 per workspace, once you link an API organization and the subscription is at least 7 days old. OpenAI says these credits do not cover Codex used through an API key and do not raise your signed-in Codex limits.

What is the catch with a 1-seat Business plan?

Seat reductions only take effect at the next billing cycle, or the next annual term on yearly billing, and do not trigger an automatic refund. Merging your personal ChatGPT workspace into Business cannot be undone. And starting in December, plan usage is shared across ChatGPT and Codex on Business, the same as Plus and Pro.
