December is a separate change

Instant chat stays outside that pool. A small group may see it early. The article still lists no token budget and no exact date.

What we couldn't confirm
  • The size of the correction. OpenAI has not published how much changed (as of Nov 25). Do not plan the week on a percentage from a screenshot thread.
  • The start date. GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol launched Nov 17, so the miss cannot be older than that. OpenAI has not said all eight days were affected.
  • Past usage vs new requests. "You may notice that you are reaching your usage limit sooner" reads like a forward change. The email does not say that recorded usage was restated.
  • A public OpenAI post. On Nov 25 the changelog's latest entry is still Codex CLI 0.172.0 from Nov 24, about terminal features. The pricing page does not mention a correction. We did not find a post from @thsottiaux about this email. His Nov 24 post is the Business reset and Fast promo only.
  • What "no extra charge" means for Business Fast. Not on the pricing page or the help article we read.
  • Pro weekly drains. User reports only. We did not read anyone's usage dashboard.
  • Whether your Nov 12 reset is still live. Expiry is per account. Open the Usage page.

FAQ

5 questions

Did OpenAI raise the Codex subscription price?

The email says the subscription price is not changing. Plus is still $20, Pro is still $100, $200, and $500, Business Standard is still $25 a seat ($20 billed yearly).

How much tighter are GPT-6.2 Codex limits now?

OpenAI has not published how much changed (as of Nov 25). The email says some Astra and Sol requests, including Ultrafast, used less allowance than intended, and that you may reach the limit sooner. No percentage, no start date, no word on recorded usage.

Did the published GPT-6.2 message estimates change?

They show the same numbers as Nov 18: 6 to 50 Astra and 17 to 180 Sol local messages per 5 hours on Plus and Standard Business. Fast 2x, Ultrafast 6x on included GPT-6.2 usage.

Does Ultrafast cost more after the correction?

The published multiplier is still 6x on included allowance, and 3x on purchased credits through Feb 28, 2027. Ultrafast is on Pro $500 for both models and on Pro $200 for Sol only. Plus, Pro $100, and Business do not get it.

What should I do if Codex is out today?

Check Settings > Usage for the reset time. Redeem a banked reset if one is listed and a covered allowance is under 80%. Same-day backups, at the prices in our tool data: Claude Code Pro $20, Copilot Pro $10, Cursor Premium $30, Antigravity on Google AI Pro $19.99.
