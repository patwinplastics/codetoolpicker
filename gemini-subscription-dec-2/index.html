Gemini subscriptions in other coding tools on Dec 2: what's actually confirmed

Short answer: On Nov 20, 2026 at 1:00 AM ET, Google's Logan Kilpatrick replied "December 2nd we take the first step" after a developer asked Google to let Gemini subscriptions work in any harness. That is all Google has said in public. He did not name Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, OpenCode or any other tool, and he did not say which plans would qualify.

Posts soon treated that reply as a launch: your Gemini plan works in any coding tool on Dec 2. That is a rumor, not a product announcement. Google's Antigravity FAQ, still live today, says using third-party software with your Antigravity login violates the Terms of Service and can get an account suspended.

If you want Gemini 4 Neon this week, you already can, just not by pointing a Gemini consumer login at Claude Code. It runs in Antigravity on Google AI Pro ($19.99) and Ultra, in Copilot Pro+ and up, in Cursor on Premium and up, and through the paid Gemini API at $3/$15 per million tokens. The picker below helps you decide whether to wait or use one of those paths now.

The rules today, which have not changed

Google's Antigravity FAQ answers "Why can't I use third-party software (such as Claude Code, OpenClaw, or OpenCode) with my Antigravity login?" directly: using third-party software, tools or services to access Antigravity is a Terms of Service violation and can result in suspension or termination. It tells you to use a Gemini Enterprise or Google AI Studio API key instead. A second FAQ entry says wrapping Antigravity CLI headless mode (agy -p) inside third-party coding agents, or automating account rotation, is not supported under consumer plans.

The Additional Terms say the same thing in legal language, including an example of using OpenClaw with Antigravity OAuth. Until Google publishes a change, treating Dec 2 as permission to point a consumer Google AI login at Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, OpenCode or a homemade wrapper is a bet against the current rules, not a documented feature.

That API-key path is real today. It is just not your $19.99 or $99.99 subscription quota. You pay Gemini list prices per token.

What we couldn't confirm

  • What the "first step" actually is. It could be one partner, a private preview, a docs or terms change, a waitlist, or something else. Google has not said.
  • Which tools get access first. Hermes is a rumor, not a naming by Google or Nous in response to Logan's reply. Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Pi and OpenCode are wishes in other people's posts.
  • Which plans and what quota. A $19.99 Google AI Pro plan may or may not be enough outside Antigravity. There are no published numbers.
  • Whether the Antigravity terms will change on Dec 2. They had not changed as of this check. A first step could leave the current ban in place for most tools.
  • Whether Copilot or Cursor will ever bill a Gemini subscription instead of their own credits. Nothing public says they will. Today those tools bill Neon at API list price on their own plans.

FAQ

Will my Gemini subscription work in Claude Code or Codex on December 2?

Google has not said that. On Nov 20, 2026 at 1:00 AM ET, Logan Kilpatrick replied "December 2nd we take the first step" after Kun Chen asked Google to allow Gemini subscriptions in any harness. He did not name Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, OpenCode or any other tool, and he did not say which Google AI plans would qualify. As of Nov 22, 2026 there is no official product announcement.

Can I use my Google AI Pro or Ultra login in a third-party coding tool today?

Not under current consumer terms. Google's Antigravity FAQ, still live on Nov 22, 2026, says using third-party software such as Claude Code, OpenClaw or OpenCode with your Antigravity login is a Terms of Service violation and can lead to account suspension or termination. The official path for a third-party agent is a Gemini Enterprise or Google AI Studio API key, which is billed per token, not against your Google AI plan quota.

Where can I use Gemini 4 Neon today?

In Google Antigravity on paid Google AI Pro ($19.99) and Ultra, where it is the default model (512K context) and can plan in Orchestrator with Hydrogen as the executor; in Google AI Studio on Pro or Ultra; in the paid Gemini API at $3 input / $0.15 cached / $15 output per million tokens; in GitHub Copilot on Pro+, Max, Business and Enterprise at that list price; and in Cursor on Premium and up, billed at the same list price from prepaid credit (legacy Cursor plans use the Other Models pool). Neon is not on Antigravity Free or Plus, Copilot Pro or Free, or Cursor Plus.

Is December 1 the same as December 2?

No. December 1, 2026 is a confirmed Gemini API price change: Gemini 4 Carbon and Argon go from $2/$10 per million tokens to $4/$20. Copilot and Cursor docs follow that date. December 2 is only Logan Kilpatrick's "first step" date for Gemini subscriptions in other harnesses, with no public details.

Should I cancel Cursor or Claude Code and wait for December 2?

No. There is no partner list, no plan list and no quota rule. If you already have Google AI Pro or Ultra, you can use Neon in Antigravity today. If you already have Copilot Pro+ or Cursor Premium, Neon is already in those tools, billed at API list price against that tool's credits, not against your Gemini subscription. Waiting is reasonable only if you can keep working in a tool you already have.
