SWE-2 Flash vs SWE-2: when the cheap model is enough

Short answer: use SWE-2 Flash for everyday work in Devin Desktop and the Devin CLI (reviews, docs, routine features), and keep SWE-2 for harder tasks. Flash does not run in Devin Cloud. Both are free on self-serve Desktop and CLI right now: SWE-2 through Nov 30, 2026, Flash until Dec 4, 2026 at 18:00 UTC. After those windows, Flash is $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output, versus $3.00 and $15.00 for SWE-2. On our medium-task estimate that is about $0.04 vs about $1.23. Cognition's blog says Flash is one-fifth the price and 3x the speed. The 3x figure is a simulated replay. The one-fifth does not match these self-serve list rates, which are one-thirtieth. Medium effort is on the Free plan. High and Max need a paid plan.

Checked Nov 6, 2026 on Cognition's Nov 4 announcement, Devin Desktop model docs (the docs page itself says rates as of Nov 5), and devin.ai/pricing. Plan prices on that pricing page were unchanged (Free $0, Pro $20, Max $200, Teams $80 + $40/seat). Our own math is labeled as an estimate. Benchmark scores below are Cognition's, not an independent eval.

What shipped, and who can use it

On Nov 4, 2026 Cognition launched SWE-2 Flash, a smaller coding model for the work it says agents already do a lot of: automations, code review, feature development, and documentation. It is built on Inkling-Small from Thinking Machines: 276B total parameters, 12B active. SWE-2 is 2.8T total, 104B active.

Where it runs is the first filter. Devin's docs say Flash is in Devin Desktop and Devin CLI only. It is not in Devin Cloud. If the job has to run while your laptop is off, Flash is not the model. Cloud sessions still use SWE-2 or another model your plan allows.

Effort is the second filter. Flash comes in Medium, High, and Max. Medium is on the Free plan. High and Max need a paid plan. After the free window, docs say all three variants cost the same token rates.

The arithmetic, so you can check it: 10% of 1.5M at the input rate, 90% of 1.5M at the cached-token rate, and 0.025M at the output rate. For Flash that cached rate is the cache-read price ($0.01/M). For SWE-2 the docs publish one cache-input price ($0.30/M) and no separate cache-write price, so we use $0.30 for the cached 90%.

  • Flash: 0.15M x $0.10 + 1.35M x $0.01 + 0.025M x $0.50 = $0.015 + $0.0135 + $0.0125 = $0.041, about $0.04.
  • SWE-2: 0.15M x $3.00 + 1.35M x $0.30 + 0.025M x $15.00 = $0.45 + $0.405 + $0.375 = $1.23.

That is about 30 times, not 5. Each self-serve list rate is one-thirtieth of SWE-2's ($0.10 vs $3.00, $0.01 vs $0.30, $0.50 vs $15.00), so the estimate scales the same way.

If the uncached 0.15M tokens are also written to cache once, Flash's $0.125/M cache-write rate adds about $0.02, for about $0.06. SWE-2's docs list a cache-input rate and don't list a separate cache-write rate, so we can't add the same line for SWE-2.

Why the blog says one-fifth

Cognition's announcement says Flash is "one-fifth the price" of SWE-2. It doesn't say which price. The self-serve list rates are one-thirtieth, so that claim does not describe the sticker on the self-serve tab.

On the enterprise (ACU) tab of the same docs page, SWE-2 is $0.50/M input, $2.50/M output, and $0.05/M cache reads through Dec 31, 2026 (Pacific time), then the $3 / $15 / $0.30 list price. Flash's listed rate is exactly one-fifth of that promo ($0.10, $0.50, and $0.01). Enterprise Flash is an extra 25% off until Nov 18, 2026 at 18:00 UTC, so it is cheaper than one-fifth during that discount. Our reading, not Cognition's explanation: "one-fifth" lines up with enterprise promo SWE-2 versus Flash's list rate. Self-serve customers should budget the one-thirtieth list rates after the free windows, not the blog's fraction.

We couldn't confirm whether "free" means the model also skips your daily and weekly quota, or only that extra usage bills at $0. The docs introduce the rate table as extra-usage pricing and say quota consumption can differ. Set a spend limit before Dec 4 if you expect to go past the allowance.

FAQ

What is SWE-2 Flash?

Cognition launched SWE-2 Flash on Nov 4, 2026. It is a smaller coding model (276B total parameters, 12B active, based on Inkling-Small) for Devin Desktop and Devin CLI. It does not run in Devin Cloud. SWE-2 is 2.8T total and 104B active. Cognition says Flash is about 3x the speed and one-fifth the price of SWE-2. The speed figure is a simulated replay at 200 vs 60 tokens per second, not a live production benchmark.

Is SWE-2 Flash free?

Devin docs say SWE-2 Flash is free until Dec 4, 2026 at 18:00 UTC. After that, Medium, High and Max all cost $0.10 per million input tokens, $0.01 per million cache reads, $0.125 per million cache writes, and $0.50 per million output tokens. Full SWE-2 is separately free on self-serve Desktop and CLI through Nov 30, 2026, and that free window does not apply to Devin Cloud. After Nov 30, SWE-2 list pricing is $3.00 per million input, $15.00 per million output, and $0.30 per million cache input.

Does SWE-2 Flash work in Devin Cloud?

No. Devin's models docs say SWE-2 Flash is available in Devin Desktop and Devin CLI, not in Devin Cloud. Cloud agents still need another model, such as SWE-2 or a frontier model on a paid plan.

When should I use Flash instead of SWE-2?

Use Flash for everyday agent work on Desktop or CLI: reviews, docs, and routine features. Cognition's own evals put Flash essentially even with SWE-2 on FrontierCode 2.0 (24.0 vs 23.8) and SWE-Bench Pro v2 Hard (89.9 vs 90.6). Keep SWE-2 for harder tasks. SWE-2 scores higher on Terminal-Bench 4.0 (27.3 vs 20.0) and DeepSWE 1.1 (73.0 vs 60.1). Those are vendor evals. Through Nov 30, 2026 both are free on self-serve Desktop and CLI, so the quality gap costs nothing until then.

How much does one task cost after the free window?

On our medium-task estimate (1.5M input tokens with 90% cached, plus 25K output tokens, cache writes left out), SWE-2 Flash is about $0.04 and SWE-2 list pricing is about $1.23. That is about 30 times, because the published self-serve rates are one-thirtieth, not the one-fifth in Cognition's blog. This is an estimate. Real tasks vary, and quota use inside a plan can differ from these extra-usage rates.

Can I use SWE-2 Flash on the Free plan?

Medium effort, yes. Devin docs say SWE-2 Flash Medium is on the Free plan, and High and Max need a paid plan. All three variants share the same token price after Dec 4. We could not confirm that the Free plan includes full SWE-2. The pricing page lists free SWE-2 under Pro.

Are the SWE-2 Flash weights public?

No. Cognition says it is not sharing the weights publicly yet, because the same cybersecurity skill that raised ExploitBench from 11.3% on the Inkling-Small base to 34.8% on Flash can be misused. It says it is building a program to share the weights with trusted industry partners and research labs. That is from the Nov 4, 2026 blog. Program details were not published as of Nov 6.
