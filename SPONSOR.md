# Sponsor CodeToolPicker

**CodeToolPicker** (https://patwinplastics.github.io/codetoolpicker/) helps developers and vibe coders pick an AI coding tool through a 60-second quiz and a live side-by-side comparison of Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude Code, Windsurf, Lovable, and Bolt.new.

## Audience
- Developers, indie hackers, and "vibe coders" actively deciding which AI coding tool to pay for, so they're close to buying
- Interested in AI agents, dev tools, and game/side-project building
- Arrive from search ("Cursor vs Copilot", "best AI coding tool"), AI chatbot citations, and X

## Editorial policy
**Rankings are not for sale.** Sponsorship and affiliate relationships never change rankings, recommendations, quiz logic, or comparison data. Every sponsored placement is labeled "Sponsored" and every affiliate link is disclosed. Sponsors get visibility, not verdicts: if a sponsor's pricing or product changes, we report it the same way we would for anyone else. This policy is shown on the homepage and on every comparison page.
