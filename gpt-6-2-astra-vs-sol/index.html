Short answer: make Sol your default and save Astra for the hard stuff, like a bug that crosses services. On ChatGPT Plus, OpenAI's own estimates mean one Astra message uses about three Sol messages of your 5-hour limit, and in the API Astra is 4x the price per token. OpenAI's own coding benchmark puts Sol within half a point of Astra at about a fifth of the cost.

Two things people miss: GPT-6.2 is not in regular ChatGPT Chat (it's in Work and Codex), and it's not in Cursor. Cursor's model list still has no GPT-6 models at all. GitHub Copilot does have both, from Pro+ up.

Both 6.2 models go about 11 to 20% further than the ones they replace. The ratio between them is what matters: about 2.8 to 3.6 Sol messages for every Astra message. If you spend a whole 5-hour window on Astra, you'd have gotten roughly three times as many turns out of Sol.

How to switch in Codex
  1. Update the ChatGPT desktop app (Menu, Check for Updates) and the Codex CLI. OpenAI says the models may not show until you do.
  2. In the Codex app, pick the model in the model picker. In the CLI, type /model.
  3. In ChatGPT, open Work, not Chat. New Work chats start on Sol at Light reasoning on personal plans.
  4. Not there yet? It's a rollout. OpenAI says availability depends on plan, workspace settings and rollout progress.

What we couldn't confirm
  • Business plan access. Docs say yes, the launch post leaves it out, and some Business users reported not seeing it on launch day (OpenAI forum, via third-party coverage). Treat as rolling out.
  • When Cursor gets GPT-6.2. No date from Cursor or OpenAI. Cursor still shows GPT-5.6 as its newest OpenAI line.
  • Benchmarks. FrontierCode, Agents' Last Exam and the cost-per-task claims are OpenAI's numbers. Independent coding-agent results weren't out yet when we checked.
  • GPT-5.6 retirement. A forum rumor says December. We found no OpenAI page with a date.
  • Weekly limits. OpenAI doesn't publish them, only the 5-hour ranges.

FAQ

5 questions

Should I use GPT-6.2 Astra or GPT-6.2 Sol for coding?

Start on Sol for everyday coding, pull request review and revisions. Switch to Astra for hard multi-step work, like tracing a bug across services. OpenAI's FrontierCode 2.0 result has Sol within 0.5 points of Astra at about one-fifth the cost (both at max effort). On Plus, each Astra message uses roughly three Sol messages of your limit.

Which ChatGPT plans get GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol?

Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Edu (Enterprise and Edu once an admin enables them). OpenAI's help pages also list Business; the launch post doesn't. Free and Go stay on GPT-6 Luna. It's in Work and Codex, not regular Chat.

Can I use GPT-6.2 in Cursor or GitHub Copilot?

Copilot yes, on Pro+, Max, Business and Enterprise at provider list price. Not on Copilot Pro ($10). Cursor no: its models page listed no GPT-6 models on Nov 18, 2026.

How much do GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol cost in the API?

Astra $8 input, $0.40 cached, $40 output per million tokens. Sol $2, $0.10, $10, unchanged from GPT-6.1 Sol. Over 272K input tokens: 2x input, 1.5x output. Batch half price. Ultrafast 3x through Feb 28, 2027, then 6x.

Is there a GPT-6.2 Luna?

No. Only Astra and Sol launched on Nov 17, 2026. GPT-6 Luna is still the newest Luna.
