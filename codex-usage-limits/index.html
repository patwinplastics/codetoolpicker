Codex usage limits explained (Nov 2026): no, it didn't go unlimited

Short answer: Codex is not unlimited, and nothing from OpenAI ever said it would be. What landed on Thursday, Nov 12 was a banked reset: a one-time refill that sits in your account until you redeem it. Your plan is the same size it was on Monday. Plus ($20) still has a 5-hour window and a weekly limit. Pro ($100, $200, $500) has only a weekly limit, and OpenAI's Codex lead says it's 10x, 20x and 50x Plus. The change that actually matters is coming in December, when ChatGPT and Codex start sharing one token-based allowance on Plus, Pro and Business. Our advice: hold the reset until you hit a wall, and check its expiry today.

Checked Nov 13, 2026 on OpenAI's Codex pricing page, the help articles on Codex resets, 5-hour and weekly windows, the December shared limit, Pro tiers, paid resets and dots, plus posts on X by Codex lead Tibo Sottiaux. Anything we couldn't confirm with OpenAI is labeled.

How the banked reset works (and when to use it)

  • Getting it doesn't refill anything. It's saved for later. History shows "Reset received" when it lands and "Reset used" when you redeem it.
  • You can't use it early. At least one allowance it covers must be below 80% remaining. Otherwise you get "Your usage does not need a reset right now" and it stays saved.
  • What it restores: Plus and Business Standard get the 5-hour and weekly allowances back. Pro 100, 200 and 500 get the weekly allowance. Go gets its monthly allowance. Anything below 100% goes back to 100%.
  • It moves your schedule. A restored period restarts, so your next weekly refresh shifts. If your weekly limit was due to refresh Friday and you redeem on Tuesday, the next refresh is the following Tuesday.
  • Where: desktop app Settings > Usage & billing, Usage settings on the web, or /usage then Redeem reset in the Codex CLI. Not in the mobile app.
  • Codex can spend it for you. The desktop setting "Allow your assistant to use resets" lets it redeem once your main 5-hour or weekly allowance is at 10% or less. Turn it off if you want to pick the moment.
  • A global reset can overlap it. If OpenAI later does an automatic reset, it doesn't give back a banked reset you already spent, and the two don't stack past 100%.

Our take: on Plus, save it for a day you hit the weekly limit, since a 5-hour wall clears itself in hours. On Pro, redeem it when your weekly allowance is low and the refresh is still days away. Either way, don't let it expire unused. Source: How resets work in Codex.

How the two windows work

  • The 5-hour window isn't a clock. A new one starts with your first Work or Codex message after the last one ended. You can hit it in 40 minutes.
  • You need room in both. A fresh 5-hour window does nothing if your weekly limit is spent.
  • It's one pool already. Codex, ChatGPT Work, ChatGPT for Excel and Workspace Agents share it today. Switching models doesn't refill it.
  • Check it: /status in the CLI, or Settings > Usage.

The real change: ChatGPT and Codex share one limit in December

OpenAI's help center: "Starting in December, Plus, Pro, and Business plans' usage allowance will be shared across ChatGPT and Codex." Usage will be measured in tokens, so a long document analysis in chat can eat into your Codex week. Instant chat won't count. OpenAI says it expects "most users won't need to wait for their usage to reset more often than they do now," and you'll see the combined number in Settings > Usage.

  • Confirmed: December, Plus, Pro and Business, token-based, Instant excluded. Source: Understanding ChatGPT and Codex usage limits.
  • Not confirmed: the Dec 1 date going around. OpenAI's page says only "starting in December." It also hasn't said how big the shared allowance will be or whether Free, Go, Enterprise or Edu change.
  • What to do: if you use Thinking models in chat a lot and lean on Codex, watch your usage page in the first December week before you buy anything. Moving routine chat to Instant should keep it off your Codex budget.

Do dots lose their extra limits on Nov 18?

Unconfirmed for Pro. Nov 18 is real for Enterprise, Edu and Healthcare workspaces: their free dots preview ends and billing begins, and dots turn on by default unless an admin opts out (OpenAI help). For Pro and Business Premium, OpenAI said on Oct 29 it would keep extended dot limits "for now," with dot usage separate from your default usage (Tibo's post). We found no OpenAI source that ends that on Nov 18.

7 ways to make your Codex limit last

  1. Use Luna or Terra for routine work. On Plus, OpenAI's estimate is 350 to 3,000 Luna messages per 5 hours vs 15 to 160 on GPT-6.1 Sol. Terra sits in between at 35 to 285. Save Sol and Astra for the hard stuff.
  2. Turn off Fast mode. It uses your included allowance at 2.5x the Standard rate. Ultrafast on Pro 500 is 8x.
  3. Run it locally. Cloud tasks can use more allowance than local messages.
  4. Trim the context. Point Codex at the files that matter, keep AGENTS.md short (nest them in big repos), and disable MCP servers you aren't using. Every MCP server adds context to every message.
  5. Start fresh after compaction loops. If you see "Context automatically compacted" with no progress, stop and start a new chat with a short summary. (It won't reset your limit, but it stops the bleed.)
  6. Keep an API key as overflow. Any plan can run extra local chats on an API key at API prices. GPT-6.1 Sol is $2 in and $10 out per million tokens. Pro and Business subscribers can claim monthly API credits for this; how that works.
  7. Don't buy an instant reset by reflex. On eligible Plus and Pro accounts it applies the moment you pay, even with usage left, can't be saved, and pulls next week's allowance forward rather than adding to it. OpenAI shows the price at checkout only, and refunds are generally not offered (source).

Tips 1 to 4 and 6 are from OpenAI's pricing FAQ and help center. The ordering and the "use a key as overflow" framing are ours.

FAQ

Is Codex unlimited now?

No. The "Codex gets unlimited usage tomorrow" post that went around on Nov 11 and 12, 2026 had no OpenAI source. What actually happened on Thursday, Nov 12: Codex lead Tibo Sottiaux said a banked reset had been activated and would land in accounts soon. That's a one-time refill you redeem yourself, not a bigger or unlimited plan. OpenAI's pricing page still says weekly limits may apply.

What is a Codex banked reset?

A free, one-time reset saved to your account. Getting one doesn't refill anything. You redeem it later in Settings > Usage & billing on desktop, Usage settings on the web, or /usage then Redeem reset in the CLI. It only works once at least one covered allowance is below 80% remaining, and it expires, so check the date shown in your account.

What does a banked reset restore?

On Plus and Business Standard, the 5-hour and weekly allowances. On Pro 100, Pro 200 and Pro 500, the weekly allowance (Pro has no 5-hour limit). On Go, the monthly allowance. Each covered allowance below 100% goes back to 100%, and that period restarts, so your next refresh date moves.

How much Codex do I get on ChatGPT Plus?

OpenAI doesn't publish a fixed number. Its estimate for Plus is 15 to 160 local GPT-6.1 Sol messages per 5-hour window, 35 to 285 on GPT-6.1 Terra, 350 to 3,000 on GPT-6 Luna and 5 to 45 on GPT-6 Astra, with weekly limits on top. Cloud tasks use more.

How much more does ChatGPT Pro get?

Pro plans have no 5-hour limit, only a weekly allowance. On Nov 8, 2026 Tibo Sottiaux posted that Pro 100 is 10x Plus, Pro 200 is 20x and Pro 500 is 50x with Ultrafast. That's from his X post; OpenAI's help page only says Pro 200 includes more than Pro 100 and Pro 500 the most.

Will ChatGPT chat start using my Codex limit on Dec 1?

OpenAI says that starting in December, Plus, Pro and Business usage will be shared across ChatGPT and Codex and measured in tokens, with Instant chat not counting. It has not published an exact date. We found no OpenAI source for Dec 1 specifically.

Do ChatGPT dots stop having extra limits on Nov 18?

Not confirmed. Nov 18 is a real date for Enterprise, Edu and Healthcare workspaces: their free dots preview ends and billing starts. For Pro and Business Premium, OpenAI said on Oct 29 that extended dot limits continue "for now," with no end date published.
