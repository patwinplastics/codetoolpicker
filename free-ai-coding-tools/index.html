Free AI coding tools (Oct 2026): what's actually free

Short answer: you can still code with AI for $0, but the free plans keep getting smaller. The big one this week: Google Antigravity Free loses Claude Sonnet 4.6 and Claude Opus 4.6 on Nov 2, 2026. After that, its free agent runs on Gemini 3.8 Flash. GitHub Copilot Free is still the best free pick for your current editor (2,000 completions a month), Codex Free works in the ChatGPT desktop app, and Kiro Free is the one free plan we found that still lets you pick a Claude model.

Every free plan below was checked on the vendor's official pricing page or docs on Oct 27, 2026. Most vendors don't publish free quotas as numbers; where they don't, we say so instead of guessing.

Quick picks

  • Free completions in VS Code or JetBrains: GitHub Copilot Free. 2,000 completions a month plus a little chat and agent use.
  • Free agent in a full AI editor: Google Antigravity Free. Unlimited Tab, unlimited Command requests and a weekly agent quota.
  • Free Claude after Nov 2: Kiro Free, with Claude Sonnet 4.5 on 50 credits, rate limited.
  • Free OpenAI agent: Codex on ChatGPT Free, GPT-6 Luna in the desktop app (subject to rollout).
  • Free app builder, no coding: Bolt Free (1M tokens a month, no card) or Lovable Free (5 credits a day).
  • Students: Copilot Student is free, and Kiro gives eligible university students 1,000 credits a month for a year.

Nov 2: the end of free Claude in Antigravity

Right now, Antigravity Free is the easiest way to run Claude Opus 4.6 as a coding agent without paying. Google's Antigravity models page lists Claude Sonnet 4.6, Claude Opus 4.6 (thinking) and GPT-OSS-120b with a note: "Will be removed on November 2, 2026." Those 4.6 models are on the Free and Google AI Plus column and on Google AI Pro. Paid Google AI Pro users keep Claude through the newer Sonnet 5.5 and Opus 5.5, but Google says those are for non-trial subscriptions only, so people on a Google AI Pro free trial lose Claude on Nov 2 too.

For free users, that leaves Gemini 3.8 Flash as the only model on the list. If Claude is why you use Antigravity Free, here's where you can go:

Best free pick by what you're doing

Autocomplete while you type

Copilot Free if you want to stay in VS Code, JetBrains or another editor you already use; 2,000 completions a month works out to roughly 95 per workday over 21 workdays (our math, not a GitHub number). If you're willing to switch editors, Antigravity Free and Devin Desktop Free both have unlimited Tab completions.

An agent that edits files and runs commands

Antigravity Free has the most generous-sounding agent quota, refreshed weekly, but Google doesn't give a number. Codex Free is a good second agent if you have the ChatGPT desktop app. Copilot Free includes agent mode and the Copilot CLI on a small credit allowance. Cursor Free and Devin Desktop Free let you try their agents, but expect to hit the limit in a session or two (our estimate, since neither publishes a number).

Building an app without coding

Bolt Free gives you the most room: 1M tokens a month with hosting and databases, and you can see and edit the real project files. Lovable Free's 5 credits a day is better for small daily changes than one long build session. Our Lovable vs Bolt page covers the paid plans.

Terminal

Free terminal options are thin. Copilot Free includes the Copilot CLI, Antigravity Free includes the Antigravity CLI, and Gemini CLI runs on a free Gemini API key with Gemini 3.8 Flash. Claude Code and the Codex CLI both need a paid plan (Claude Pro, or ChatGPT Plus) or an API key.

The $0 stack (our suggestion)

Each free plan has its own separate limit, so using several at once is the honest way to get more for free:

  • Editor: Antigravity Free for unlimited Tab and the weekly agent quota, or Copilot Free inside VS Code.
  • Second agent: Codex Free in the ChatGPT desktop app for tasks when your main quota runs out.
  • Prototypes: Bolt Free or Lovable Free to get a UI on screen fast, then pull the code into your editor.

This is our suggestion, not a bundle anyone sells. None of these vendors publish how many agent tasks their free quota covers, so we can't promise how far it goes.

FAQ

What is the best free AI coding tool right now?

It depends on what you want for free. For completions in the editor you already use, GitHub Copilot Free gives 2,000 a month. For a free agent, Google Antigravity Free has unlimited Tab completions plus a weekly agent quota, and until Nov 2, 2026 that quota includes Claude Sonnet 4.6 and Opus 4.6. For building a small app without coding, Bolt Free (1M tokens a month) and Lovable Free (5 credits a day, up to 30 a month) both work. Checked Oct 27, 2026.

Is there still a free way to use Claude for coding?

Until Nov 2, 2026, yes: Google Antigravity Free includes Claude Sonnet 4.6 and Claude Opus 4.6 (thinking), and Google's model docs say both will be removed on that date. After that, the free option we found is Kiro Free, which includes Claude Sonnet 4.5 for people who sign up with a social login or AWS Builder ID, subject to rate limits, on 50 credits. GitHub Copilot Free picks the model for you, and Claude Free doesn't include Claude Code.

Is GitHub Copilot free?

Copilot Free costs $0 and includes 2,000 completions a month plus limited chat and agent use, with automatic model selection only. GitHub doesn't publish the size of the free credit allowance. It's only for individuals who don't get Copilot through an organization. Copilot Student is free for verified students, and verified teachers and popular open source maintainers may get Copilot Pro for free.

Is Cursor free?

Cursor has a Free plan with limited Agent and Chat requests. Cursor doesn't publish the number. Free and the $8 Plus plan don't include third-party models such as Claude, GPT or Gemini; those start on Premium at $30 a month and are billed at API list price.

Is Gemini CLI still free?

Not with a Google sign-in. Since June 18, 2026, Gemini CLI no longer serves free, Google AI Pro or Google AI Ultra accounts that log in with Google. It still accepts a Gemini API key, and Google's API pricing page lists Gemini 3.8 Flash as free of charge on the free tier, but free-tier content is used to improve Google's products and Gemini 4 Argon isn't on the free tier.

Do free AI coding tools train on my code?

Some do by default. GitHub says it may use prompts, suggestions and code snippets from individual Copilot plans, Free included, to train models unless you opt out in your Copilot settings. Google's Gemini API free tier is marked as used to improve Google's products. Cline says usage of its free models may be used to improve those models. Cursor's privacy mode guarantees your code isn't used for training. Check each tool's settings before you paste anything private.

Lovable Free or Bolt Free: which gives you more?

They measure differently, so there's no exact match. Lovable Free gives 5 build credits a day, up to 30 a month, plus 20 Cloud credits a month. Bolt Free gives 1M tokens a month with a 300K daily cap, hosting and unlimited databases, and sites carry Bolt branding. Bolt's free plan doesn't need a card. See our Lovable vs Bolt comparison for paid plans.
