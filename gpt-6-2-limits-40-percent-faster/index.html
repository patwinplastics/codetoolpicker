FAQ

How much faster do GPT-6.2 Codex limits hit now?

OpenAI's Codex lead Tibo Sottiaux posted on Nov 25, 2026 (9:50 PM ET) that, compared to last week, you will reach your limits 40% faster on GPT-6.2 Astra and 25% faster on GPT-6.2 Sol. Other models were not affected.

Why did Codex limits change?

Tibo called it a billing mistake at the GPT-6.2 launch that incorrectly discounted Astra and Sol usage for most users. The fix means the same work uses more of your plan's allowance. OpenAI says it will not charge for the difference on past usage.

Is there compensation?

Yes. Tibo said everyone on Go, Plus, Pro and Business gets one banked reset, and posted at 2:26 AM ET on Nov 26 that it is online. A banked reset refills your main Codex allowance once, when you choose to use it, and only works once a covered allowance is under 80% left.

Is GPT-6.2 still better value than the older models?

Tibo says GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol should still go further than GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol. The Codex pricing page still estimates 6 to 50 Astra and 17 to 180 Sol local messages per 5 hours on Plus, vs 5 to 45 for GPT-6 Astra and 15 to 160 for GPT-6.1 Sol.

Does the December shared ChatGPT and Codex limit make this worse?

It is a separate change. OpenAI's help center says that starting in December, Plus, Pro and Business share one token-based allowance across ChatGPT and Codex, with Instant chat excluded. OpenAI has not published the size or the exact date.
