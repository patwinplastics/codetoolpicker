Raycast AI, in Plain Terms

  • Quick AIA single hotkey, for the small questions that pop up while you work.
  • AI ChatA built-in agent that can use your tools, with projects, memory, and screen awareness so you don't have to paste everything first.
  • AI ExtensionsConnect installed extensions so the agent can act in GitHub, Linear, and the other tools you already use.
  • Bring your own modelOn a paid plan, you can connect a Claude or ChatGPT subscription, or your own API keys, without spending Raycast AI credits. Local models work through Ollama.

Extensions Worth Installing for Coding

  • GitHubOfficial Store extension: issues, pull requests, workflows, repositories and notifications. In AI Chat, type @ and pick GitHub to mention an issue, PR, or repo.
  • LinearOfficial Store extension: create, search and update issues on Mac or Windows, plus projects, cycles, documents and menu bar notifications.
  • The rest of the StoreJira, Slack, Notion and others are listed on the Store. Extensions that don't need native code work on both macOS and Windows.

Store extensions are free to install. Raycast AI features such as Quick AI and AI Chat still need a paid plan, including on Windows.

FAQ

6 questions

Is Raycast free?

Yes. Raycast is free for personal use, and the Free plan is also available for organizational use. It includes the launcher, file search, clipboard history, snippets, window management, the calculator, five Raycast Notes, and thousands of Store extensions. Raycast AI is not included on the Free plan.

How much is Raycast Pro?

Pro is $10 a month or $96 a year, with 500 monthly AI credits and fast and powerful models. Pro+ is $20 a month or $192 a year, with 3,000 credits and all models, including top-tier. Max is $50 a month or $480 a year, with 7,500 credits and all models, including top-tier. Checked Nov 27, 2026 on raycast.com/pricing and the Manual billing page.

Does Raycast work on Windows, iPhone, or Linux?

Windows: yes, Windows 10 version 21H2 or later, and Windows 11 (x64 or ARM). The Windows launcher and Store extensions are free; Quick AI and AI Chat are part of Raycast Pro. iPhone: Raycast for iOS (iOS 18) brings AI Chat, Notes, Snippets, Quicklinks and a keyboard, synced with your Mac. Linux: Raycast says it currently has no plans to bring the app there.

What does a Mac need for the current app?

Raycast v2 requires macOS Tahoe and an Apple Silicon Mac. If you're still on macOS Sequoia or earlier, you need to upgrade macOS before installing v2. People moving from v1 should be on Raycast v1.104.16 or later so the import path works.

What is included in Raycast AI?

Paid plans add Quick AI, AI Chat, AI Extensions, custom agents, automations, and screen awareness, with monthly credits. You can also connect a Claude or ChatGPT subscription, or your own API keys, without consuming Raycast credits. Eligible new accounts can start a 7-day Pro trial with Pro models and 500 trial credits. Advanced models on Pro+ and Max are not part of that trial.

Do I still need Cursor or Copilot if I have Raycast?

Yes, if that's where you write and review code. Raycast is a launcher and a quick-ask layer, plus extensions into GitHub and Linear. It is not an AI coding IDE and it is not a hosted coding agent. Keep the editor or agent you already like, and use Raycast beside it.
